**STEP 1:** Select the language (English or Hindi) from the dropdown menu.

**STEP 2:** Select the size of the training corpus. Larger corpora generally lead to better accuracy.

**STEP 3:** Select the algorithm for training the POS tagger (HMM or CRF).

**STEP 4:** Select the context feature (bigram or trigram) to use for training.

**STEP 5:** Click the 'Check Accuracy' button to view the resulting accuracy for your chosen configuration.

**STEP 6 (Optional):** Use the interactive demo in the result panel to see example sentences with their POS tags for the selected language.

**OUTPUT:**
- The accuracy of the POS tagger for the selected configuration is shown.
- Example sentences with their POS tags are available in the interactive demo for better understanding.

## Core Textbooks

#### 1. **Speech and Language Processing** _(Highly Recommended)_

**Authors**: Daniel Jurafsky & James H. Martin  
**Edition**: 3rd Edition (2023)  
**Focus**: Comprehensive coverage of POS tagging algorithms  
**Link**: [Online Version](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/)  
**Relevant Chapters**:

- Chapter 8: Part-of-Speech Tagging
- Chapter 9: Hidden Markov Models

#### 2. **Foundations of Statistical Natural Language Processing**

**Authors**: Christopher D. Manning & Hinrich Schütze  
**Publisher**: MIT Press (1999)  
**Focus**: Statistical methods in NLP including POS tagging  
**ISBN**: 978-0262133609

#### 3. **Natural Language Processing with Python**

**Authors**: Steven Bird, Ewan Klein & Edward Loper  
**Publisher**: O'Reilly Media  
**Focus**: Practical NLP implementation using NLTK  
**Online**: [NLTK Book](https://www.nltk.org/book/)  
**Relevant Chapter**: [Chapter 5 - Categorizing and Tagging Words](https://www.nltk.org/book/ch05.html)

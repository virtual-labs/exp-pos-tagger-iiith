What is POS Tagging?

Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging is the process of assigning a grammatical category (such as noun, verb, adjective, etc.) to each word in a sentence. It is a fundamental task in Natural Language Processing (NLP) and helps computers understand the structure and meaning of text.

  • POS tagging is word-level: each word gets a tag.
  • Chunking is phrase-level: groups of words are labeled as phrases (e.g., noun phrase).

POS tagging is used in applications like grammar checking, information extraction, and machine translation.

POS Tag Legend

  • NOUN: Noun
  • VERB: Verb
  • ADJ: Adjective
  • ADV: Adverb
  • PRON: Pronoun
  • DET: Determiner
  • ADP: Adposition
  • CONJ: Conjunction
  • NUM: Numeral
  • PRT: Particle
  • .: Punctuation
